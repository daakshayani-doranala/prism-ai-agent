# Portfolio-Worthy Experience Stories

> These are **experience narratives**, not standalone public demos, and should not reveal confidential production details. PRISM itself will be the independent hands-on AI agent portfolio project.

## Case study A — Making contact-center conversations more intelligent
**Problem:** At scale, customer conversations create manual after-call work and large review volumes.  
**Product opportunity:** Automated disposition, AI-supported audit evaluation, summaries, and assistance to staff.  
**Daakshayani's contribution:** Product requirements and delivery support for AI-enabled features; stakeholder alignment and validation of operational fit.  
**Business relevance:** Less repetitive work, improved consistency of review, more useful interaction insights; specific impact values require verification.  
**Product thinking shown:** Human-in-the-loop evaluation, definition of success, accuracy vs. operational usability.

## Case study B — Call branding and customer trust
**Problem:** People are less likely to answer an unfamiliar number.  
**Product opportunity:** Branded caller identification with measured pilot outcomes.  
**Daakshayani's contribution:** Vendor-facing coordination, defining test/control group work, tracking performance and facilitating internal follow-through.  
**Business relevance:** Better-informed decisions about whether branding improves engagement.  
**Product thinking shown:** Experiment design, integration dependencies, stakeholder communication.

## Case study C — Multi-channel business messaging
**Problem:** Customers and business users communicate across SMS, WhatsApp and RCS, each with different experiences and authorization requirements.  
**Daakshayani's contribution:** Coordinating requirements, consent behaviors, handoff use cases, vendors and operations alignment.  
**Business relevance:** Consistent, compliant, customer-friendly communication.  
**Product thinking shown:** End-to-end journeys, dependencies and compliance-aware requirements.

## Case study D — Scaling global communication operations
**Problem:** A successful communication workflow in one region may not fit another region's language, hours, queue structure or support process.  
**Daakshayani's contribution:** Gathering regional requirements, coordinating testing and signoffs, and aligning operations with engineering and QA teams.  
**Business relevance:** More predictable rollouts and better local fit.  
**Product thinking shown:** Global/local tradeoffs, exception paths, UAT planning and cross-functional delivery.

## Case study E — Operational visibility and reporting
**Problem:** Teams need common definitions of volume, queue performance, agent activity and service health.  
**Daakshayani's contribution:** Coordinating Tableau dashboard integration and reporting requirements, clarifying what should be measured, and communicating insights to stakeholders.  
**Business relevance:** Better visibility and informed operational decisions.  
**Product thinking shown:** KPI definitions, data literacy and stakeholder reporting.
