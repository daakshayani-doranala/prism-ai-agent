# Selected Product Initiatives — Detailed Knowledge for PRISM

All entries below are professional **high-level descriptions**, not confidential technical designs. Her contribution usually spans requirements, stakeholder alignment, vendor collaboration, product execution, testing and reporting; she should not be portrayed as the sole implementer or model developer.

## 1. AI-powered communication and contact-center features
Worked on initiatives involving automatic call disposition, AI audit scoring, call summarization, conversational AI, chatbot/voice-agent journeys, and agent assistance. These address common operational problems: repetitive after-call work, consistency of interaction review, retrieving actionable conversation context, and more efficient routing or escalation. Relevant product responsibilities include feature requirements, evaluation criteria, business feedback, release coordination, and assessing where human review remains necessary.

## 2. TransUnion and branded calling
Collaborated on branded-caller-ID work, including vendor coordination, organizing test/control populations, and analyzing call engagement and answer-rate effects. Call branding sits at the intersection of customer trust, contactability, carrier/vendor integration, experimentation, and business performance. Her cover letter references a pilot across **300+ phone numbers**, but that number should be released publicly only with approval. Brand registration and authentication discussions also involved understanding potential overlap among provider capabilities.

## 3. Twilio and communication-system integrations
Coordinated with Twilio-facing teams and internal stakeholders on communication workflows, voice/chat experiences, routing, testing, business messaging, and number readiness. She has helped clarify which product owns a particular requirement, what needs vendor input, and how business behavior should be validated end to end. This is integration coordination and product delivery experience, **not a claim she personally wrote Twilio API integrations**.

## 4. RCS, SMS, WhatsApp and business messaging
Worked on customer communication initiatives involving opt-in/opt-out behavior, messaging consent, A2P/RCS business-use requirements, campaign content and compliance alignment, and agent/customer interactions. This work required coordination among product, operations, vendors and relevant reviewers so customer messages could be usable, appropriately authorized, and aligned with platform behavior. Avoid publishing internal registration records, identifiers, consent links or compliance-sensitive drafts.

## 5. Voice, IVR, routing and customer journeys
Worked with call trees, language and skills routing, callback behavior, agent availability, live chat handoff, customer intent, virtual assistant interactions and after-hours behavior. Requirements often need to reflect how regional support teams actually operate, including fallback paths and edge cases. Her résumé reports an approximate **15% improvement** in routing accuracy/response-time outcomes; keep this figure out of the public version unless personally verified and authorized.

## 6. Global programs
Collaborated across **US, Canada, UK, Germany, Brazil, and MEA-related operations** on different product rollouts, including contact-center configurations, region-specific business hours, role/access needs, language/routing differences, testing, and production readiness. ## 7. MiOrg and business process automation
Contributed to role-based access, provisioning, organizational or operational workflow automation, asset and termination flows, and cross-system changes. This required understanding downstream dependencies, approval paths, exceptions, and usability for teams that depend on these systems daily.

## 8. Analytics and product reporting
Worked on reporting requirements and Tableau integrations for volume, queue health, agent activity, abandonment, regional trends, and operational metrics. Her contribution involves defining useful metrics, validating definitions, helping teams interpret performance, and communicating how data connects to decisions. Do not imply sole dashboard engineering where her documented role is coordination/integration.

## 9. Agile product delivery and cross-team execution
Participates in or facilitates daily standups, sprint planning, refinement, sprint reviews, risk/dependency follow-ups, UAT handoffs, issue triage and release-readiness discussions. Creates clear Jira stories and acceptance criteria and communicates status across business, engineering, QA and outside providers. Her strength is connecting implementation detail to business intention and keeping action items moving.

## 10. Continuous product improvement mindset
She tends to ask what is changing for the end user, how to prove the change works, what might fail, who must approve it, and how to make status easy for stakeholders to understand. This is a synthesis of her recurring work questions and should be validated with her before being presented as her own personal statement.
