# My Story — Beyond the Résumé

## Where the curiosity started
Growing up in India, Daakshayani observed how her family's business began making more deliberate decisions using data rather than relying only on instinct. That experience gave her an early appreciation for the intersection of information, judgment, and commercial impact.

She later studied Electronics and Communication Engineering at CVR College of Engineering, then built experience in quality assurance and analytics support at OpenText. Working across testing, product releases, and stakeholder expectations helped her understand how decisions made early in a product lifecycle affect the final user experience.

## A bigger step
She moved to the United States to pursue a Master of Science in Business Analytics and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Texas at Dallas. Her path reflects a recurring motivation: learning how to use data and technology to solve practical business problems, not simply understanding tools in isolation.

## Finding the product role
At Copart, she moved deeper into enterprise product work. She became involved in the operational realities behind customer-facing technology: what a caller experiences, where a conversation should be routed, how a business team defines success, how vendors integrate, what a tester needs to validate, what belongs on a dashboard, and what must be ready before a global rollout.

That breadth changed her view of product analysis. A feature is not 'done' simply because a requirement exists or a ticket closes. It matters whether the right people understand it, the workflow works in practice, the release is validated, and the intended business benefit can be assessed.

## Her mindset (interpretation for review)
Her working history suggests someone who is curious about mechanisms, willing to learn unfamiliar technical concepts, comfortable following up across teams, and attentive to the details that can block delivery. She frequently seeks clearer requirements, more precise communication, and better presentation of technical work to nontechnical audiences.

This is **an interpretation of demonstrated working patterns**, not a clinical personality assessment, verified third-party recommendation, or statement that she never makes mistakes.

## A human one-line summary
Daakshayani's story is about turning curiosity into execution: moving from an interest in data-driven decisions to helping global teams deliver products that make complicated customer and operational workflows easier to manage.
